# Directions — 3D

Six directions built in three dimensions, rendered or physically made. Shot recipe names
refer to [shots.md](../shots.md); grid terms to [composition.md](../composition.md).

**For every 3D direction:** name the material (clay, matte plastic, felt, paper), the
light setup (a studio softbox, a sun), and the lens: a long lens (orthographic feel)
keeps toy-like scenes tidy; a wide lens makes them feel huge. A camera that is not
stated defaults to a centered three-quarter view, which is why so many 3D images look
alike.

**Fields.** *Palette* hexes are approximate starting points: a brand's mandated colors
replace the nearest role, and the rest are tuned around them. *Staging depth* names the
row of the depth table in [staging.md](../staging.md) ("When to stage, and how deep"):
clay, soft 3D, low-poly and paper craft are **soft-3D**; isometric is **isometric**;
miniature is **full**, with camera and light from [optics-light.md](../optics-light.md).

## Clay

**Feels:** warm, handmade, funny. **Use for:** characters, explainers, kids, social,
friendly brands. **Not for:** luxury, serious or technical subjects.

- **Palette:** saturated but soft; three to five clay colors. Approx.: cream backdrop
  `#F2E6D3` (dominant) · tomato red `#E4573D` (secondary) · teal `#3A9D9B`
  (secondary) · mustard `#E8B23A` (accent) · warm brown shadow `#6B4A3A` (shadow).
- **Light:** a soft key from above-left, a warm bounce; soft contact shadows.
- **Medium & texture:** fingerprints, slight tool marks, uneven edges, matte surfaces.
- **Shots:** eye-level medium on a small set, low-angle hero for a character, high-angle
  down over a tabletop.
- **Composition:** a small set with a visible floor and backdrop; characters with
  exaggerated proportions.
- **Staging depth:** soft-3D — tabletop sizes, contact on the set floor, one soft key,
  the clay's matte finish and marks, one emphasis.
- **Type:** rounded bold sans, or letters modelled in clay.
- **Variants:**
  - *Plasticine stop-motion* — fingerprints, wobbly edges, felt and wire props.
  - *Smooth clay render* — cleaner digital clay, soft shading, no fingerprints.
  - *Felt and wool* — needle-felted fuzz, knitted textures, stitches.
- **Engines:** every family does this well. Nano Banana for a recurring character
  across scenes. Midjourney — gorgeous; `--s` moderate. State "fingerprints visible"
  or it goes smooth plastic.
- **Slop risks:** shiny plastic called clay, the same big-eyed character, a too-clean
  set.

## Soft 3D

**Feels:** friendly, toy-like, polished. **Use for:** app marketing, icon scenes,
onboarding, fintech and productivity brands. **Not for:** gritty, real or heritage.

- **Palette:** pastels or candy colors on a matching background; one accent. Approx.:
  peach backdrop `#FFD9C7` (dominant) · mint `#BDEBD9` (secondary) · butter `#FFE8A3`
  (secondary) · coral `#FF6B57` (accent) · soft rose-brown shadow `#C9A99A` (shadow).
- **Light:** large soft studio light, gentle ambient occlusion in the creases, soft
  shadow.
- **Medium & texture:** matte or satin plastic, rounded edges everywhere, subtle
  subsurface glow.
- **Shots:** three-quarter product, isometric, flat frontal.
- **Composition:** one hero object plus zero to three small satellites. Floating is
  licensed here: objects may hover, as long as a soft shadow beneath each one marks
  where the ground is.
- **Staging depth:** soft-3D — sizes relative to the hero, contact or licensed floating
  with a ground shadow, one large soft light, the plastic's finish, one emphasis.
- **Type:** rounded geometric sans.
- **Variants:**
  - *Pastel matte* — soft pastels, matte surfaces.
  - *Inflated* — puffy, balloon-like forms with pinched seams.
  - *Glossy toy* — shiny vinyl-toy surfaces with crisp highlights.
  - *Animated-feature 3D character* — a stylized character as in a feature-length 3D
    animated film: big readable shapes, expressive eyes and brows, soft subsurface skin,
    hair and cloth with real weight, a key and a rim like a film frame, a pose with a
    clear line of action. Describe proportions and materials; never name a studio or a
    film. A recurring character needs a reference sheet (`mw-image-prompt`).
- **Engines:** a real product shown as soft 3D keeps its own shape and label from the
  user's photo, never redrawn. Midjourney and Flux strongest on materials. GPT-Image for
  exact layouts with labels. Name the background color or it defaults to a gradient.
- **Slop risks:** floating random shapes, purple-pink everything, coins and rockets for
  any finance or launch theme.

## Isometric

**Feels:** systematic, explanatory, tidy. **Use for:** how-it-works diagrams,
architecture of a product, process scenes, offices and cities. **Not for:** emotion,
portraits.

- **Palette:** a limited palette with one color per material or system part. Approx.:
  light gray floor `#ECEFF3` (dominant) · system blue `#3A7BD5` (secondary) · green
  `#4CAF7D` (secondary) · orange `#F28C28` (accent) · shadow-side gray `#9AA5B1`
  (shadow).
- **Light:** one consistent direction across the scene; shadows on one side only.
- **Medium & texture:** clean 3D or flat-shaded vector.
- **Shots:** isometric (true 30° axes, no perspective).
- **Composition:** a diorama block or a floor tile; elements on a grid; paths or wires
  connecting them; room for labels.
- **Staging depth:** isometric — sizes kept consistent on the grid, one light direction
  with shadows on one side only, one emphasis; no perspective, no lens.
- **Type:** small sans labels with leader lines.
- **Variants:**
  - *Diorama* — a cut-out block of the world, with soil or floor layers visible.
  - *Isometric room* — one room, cut open, furniture and people.
  - *Tech explainer* — servers, devices, data paths as objects.
- **Engines:** models often add perspective; state "isometric projection, parallel
  lines stay parallel, no vanishing point". GPT-Image best with labels.
- **Slop risks:** perspective sneaking in, glowing data lines, too many tiny objects.

## Low-poly

**Feels:** geometric, light, game-like. **Use for:** backgrounds, game art, data and
abstract metaphors. **Not for:** realism or warmth.

- **Palette:** gradients across facets; two or three hues. Approx.: deep teal `#0F4C5C`
  (dominant) · sea green `#2A9D8F` (secondary) · sand `#E9C46A` (accent) · night
  shadow `#0B2530` (shadow).
- **Light:** one direction; each facet one flat tone.
- **Medium & texture:** visible triangles, sharp facet edges.
- **Shots:** wide environmental, isometric, three-quarter.
- **Composition:** simple silhouettes; landscapes as layered faceted planes.
- **Staging depth:** soft-3D — silhouette sizes, contact with the ground plane, one
  light that sets each facet's tone, one emphasis.
- **Type:** geometric sans.
- **Variants:**
  - *Flat-shaded* — clean facets, matte.
  - *Faceted gradient* — color shifting across facets, often on a dark background.
- **Engines:** all fine; state the facet count range ("a few hundred triangles").
- **Slop risks:** smooth shading that hides the facets, polygon wireframe overlays.

## Miniature

**Feels:** charming, toy-world, curious. **Use for:** city and process scenes,
storytelling, logistics and travel. **Not for:** exact scale or portraits.

- **Palette:** bright, slightly saturated; toy colors. Approx.: toy grass `#5DAE4B`
  (dominant) · sky blue `#79C2E8` (secondary) · road gray `#8A8C8E` (secondary) · tram
  red `#D93B30` (accent) · sharp shadow `#2E3A2C` (shadow).
- **Light:** bright sun or a hard model-railway lamp.
- **Medium & texture:** a strip of sharp focus with strong blur above and below (the
  tilt-shift look), or model-railway materials (static grass, painted figures).
- **Shots:** high-angle down (30–60°), aerial.
- **Composition:** the in-focus band across the frame on a third; many small subjects
  doing small things.
- **Staging depth:** full — the chosen scale (1:1 tabletop or a 1:87 world), camera
  height, the narrow focus band that makes it read small, one hard light.
- **Type:** friendly sans.
- **Variants:**
  - *Tilt-shift photo* — a real scene shot from above with a narrow focus band.
  - *Model railway diorama* — a physical model with painted figures and props.
- **Engines:** describe the focus band and the camera height; all families can do it.
- **Slop risks:** blur everywhere, no clear in-focus subject.

## Paper craft

**Feels:** crafted, layered, warm. **Use for:** editorial, seasonal campaigns, kids,
sustainability. **Not for:** high-tech or photoreal.

- **Palette:** colored paper stock: muted or bright, with white highlights. Approx.:
  sage paper `#9DB89A` (dominant) · cream `#F1E9DA` (secondary) · terracotta `#D0754F`
  (accent) · deep forest `#2F4A3A` (shadow).
- **Light:** a soft side light that shows the edges and casts small shadows between
  layers.
- **Medium & texture:** paper fiber, cut edges, slight curl, real depth between layers.
- **Shots:** flat frontal (layers stacked toward camera), three-quarter.
- **Composition:** depth by layers: foreground, middle, back; frame within a frame.
- **Staging depth:** soft-3D — layer sizes and the gaps between them, one soft side
  light casting small shadows between layers, paper fiber and cut edges, one emphasis.
- **Type:** cut-paper letters or a friendly serif.
- **Variants:**
  - *Layered cut paper* — stacked silhouettes with shadows between.
  - *Origami* — folded forms, crisp creases.
  - *Pop-up* — a book spread with standing elements.
- **Engines:** all families good; state the shadow depth between layers.
- **Slop risks:** flat vector pretending to be paper, no shadows between layers.
