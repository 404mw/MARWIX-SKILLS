# Directions — Eras

Seven directions defined by a time, real or imagined. Shot recipe names refer to
[shots.md](../shots.md); grid terms to [composition.md](../composition.md).

**For every era:** "retro" or "futuristic" alone gives the model's average of the
period. Name the decade or the strand, its medium (photo, print, 3D), and two or three
period details (materials, colors, type) — then keep modern objects out of frame, or
make the anachronism the point. Some strands are revivals, not the decade itself:
synthwave and vaporwave are 2010s revivals of 80s and 90s imagery, and Frutiger Aero
(about 2004–2013) came after Y2K. For "the real 80s", use Memphis and period photos and
print.

**Fields.** *Palette* hexes are approximate starting points: a brand's mandated colors
replace the nearest role, and the rest are tuned around them. *Staging depth* names the
row of the depth table in [staging.md](../staging.md) ("When to stage, and how deep");
an era follows its medium — full for photo and render, painterly for painted, flat for
print and poster. Engine traits marked "(observed, small sample, 2026)" come from a
handful of renders, not a test; recheck them on the current model.

## Retro

**Feels:** nostalgic, specific. **Use for:** campaigns, merch, social, food and drink,
music. **Not for:** a brand that must feel current, unless the contrast is the idea.

- **Palette:** per decade — see variants. Approx., 70s warm: cream `#F1E3C6`
  (dominant) · burnt orange `#C8642B` (secondary) · mustard `#D9A43A` (secondary) ·
  faded teal `#4E8C88` (accent) · brown `#5C3B23` (shadow).
- **Light:** per medium of the time: flat print, warm film, neon.
- **Medium & texture:** the period's own medium: offset print, film, VHS.
- **Shots:** per variant; period photos favor eye-level medium and wide group shots;
  period ads favor low-angle hero products.
- **Composition:** period layouts — centered badges for the 50s, flowing type for the
  60s–70s, grids and gradients for the 80s.
- **Staging depth:** follows the medium — flat for period print and ads; full for a
  period photograph (period camera height and light, period objects at real sizes).
- **Type:** the decade's typefaces described by shape (rounded bold script, groovy
  swash display, chrome bevel, distressed grunge).
- **Variants:**
  - *50s atomic* — mint, coral, cream; starbursts and boomerangs; optimistic ad
    illustration.
  - *60s mod and psychedelic* — mod: bold geometric color blocks, op-art; psychedelic:
    swirling saturated color, flowing type.
  - *70s warm* — burnt orange, mustard, brown, faded teal; warm film; rounded type;
    sun-faded.
  - *80s Memphis and synthwave revival* — Memphis (the early-80s design group):
    squiggles, terrazzo, primary pastels; synthwave (a 2010s revival of 80s imagery):
    magenta-cyan grid horizon, sunset gradient, chrome type.
  - *90s grunge and vaporwave revival* — grunge (90s): photocopy, distressed type,
    flash; vaporwave (a 2010s revival of 90s imagery): pastel pink-teal, classical
    busts, early computer UI.
- **Engines:** Midjourney best for look development; Flux for period photos. Describe
  the decade's materials, not "retro filter".
- **Slop risks:** every era merged into one, synthwave grid on everything,
  anachronisms (modern phones in a 70s scene).

## Y2K

**Feels:** glossy, optimistic, internet-pop. **Use for:** youth, fashion, music,
tech-nostalgia, beauty. **Not for:** serious, heritage or eco brands.

- **Palette:** silver chrome, baby blue, pink, lime; or aqua and grass green for
  Frutiger Aero. Approx., pop Y2K: silver chrome `#C9CED6` (dominant) · baby blue
  `#9CD3F5` (secondary) · bubblegum pink `#FF9ECF` (secondary) · lime `#C4F23A`
  (accent) · chrome shadow `#4A5160` (shadow).
- **Light:** bright, glossy specular highlights, lens flare is period-correct here (in
  moderation).
- **Medium & texture:** chrome and liquid metal, translucent plastic, bubbles, glitter,
  low-res early-web graphics.
- **Shots:** flat frontal, three-quarter product, fisheye close-up (period look).
- **Composition:** centered and symmetrical, floating objects, stickers and stars.
  Floating is licensed: objects may hover, as long as a soft shadow or reflection
  beneath marks where the ground is.
- **Staging depth:** soft-3D for chrome and plastic objects (sizes, licensed floating
  with a ground shadow, one glossy light, materials, one emphasis); full for a Y2K
  photo; flat for early-web graphics.
- **Type:** bubble letters, chrome bevel, wide futuristic sans, pixel fonts.
- **Variants:**
  - *Pop Y2K* — pink and baby blue, butterflies, glitter, flip phones, velour.
  - *Cyber chrome* — liquid silver, reflective fabrics, dark backgrounds, tech-angular
    type.
  - *Frutiger Aero (post-Y2K, about 2004–13)* — the look that followed Y2K, kept here as
    its neighbor: glossy skeuomorphic, aqua and green, sky, water, bubbles, fish,
    hopeful nature-meets-tech.
- **Engines:** a real product in a Y2K set is placed from the user's photo, never
  redrawn. Midjourney and Flux render chrome best. GPT-Image for text.
- **Slop risks:** chrome on everything, butterflies by default, mixing the three
  strands into mush.

## Futuristic

**Feels:** clean, aspirational, sci-fi. **Use for:** tech launches, AI, mobility,
energy, space. **Not for:** heritage, handmade or cozy.

- **Palette:** per variant: white and pale gray with one light color; or brass and
  teal for retro-futurism; or greens and white for solarpunk. Approx., clean sci-fi:
  white `#F5F6F7` (dominant) · pale gray `#D8DCE0` (secondary) · soft cyan `#7FD1E0`
  (accent) · cool shadow gray `#6E7781` (shadow).
- **Light:** large soft area lights, light lines built into architecture; daylight for
  solarpunk.
- **Medium & texture:** seamless surfaces, rounded architecture, glass and composite.
- **Shots:** wide environmental (a person small in a vast space), low-angle hero,
  symmetric center-punch.
- **Composition:** big negative space, strong perspective lines, one human for scale.
- **Staging depth:** full — the scale figure at real height against the architecture,
  camera height, area lights as named sources, contact.
- **Type:** wide extended sans, light weight.
- **Variants:**
  - *Clean sci-fi* — white, seamless, calm; near-future believable.
  - *Retro-futurism* — the future as imagined in the 1950s–70s: chrome rockets,
    domed cities, ray-gun curves.
  - *Solarpunk* — green cities, plants on buildings, solar panels, daylight, hopeful.
- **Engines:** Midjourney for look exploration; Flux pro for photoreal architecture.
- **Slop risks:** holograms everywhere, a blue glowing brain, purple-blue light,
  chrome humanoid robots.

## Cyberpunk

**Feels:** gritty, neon, rebellious. **Use for:** games, security, nightlife, music,
tech with attitude. **Not for:** wellness, kids, family.

- **Palette:** dark base with two neon accents (magenta and cyan is the cliché — pick
  another pair if possible: acid green and orange, red and teal). Approx.: wet asphalt
  `#0B0E12` (dominant, shadow) · dark teal-gray `#1C2A30` (secondary) · acid green
  `#A6FF3B` (accent) · sodium orange `#FF7A1A` (accent).
- **Light:** practicals only — signs, screens, wet reflections; 70% of frame dark.
- **Medium & texture:** rain, grime, cables, dense signage, reflections.
- **Shots:** ground-level, low-angle hero, wide environmental (alley),
  over-the-shoulder.
- **Composition:** layers of signs and structures; deep vertical space.
- **Staging depth:** full — practicals as the only lights, each with its source in
  frame, wet reflections under them, 70% dark share; painterly when painted.
- **Type:** condensed tech sans, signage-like.
- **Variants:**
  - *Neon noir* — night, rain, neon reflections, lone figure.
  - *Used-future daylight* — overcast day, worn tech, markets, dense and dusty.
- **Engines:** `neon` and `glowing` bring bloom on every family; if the brief wants
  crisp signs, describe "a clean bright line with a defined edge". Nano Banana holds
  the dark; GPT-Image warms and brightens night (both observed, small sample, 2026).
- **Slop risks:** magenta-cyan everything, rain plus fog plus neon plus hood, fake
  Asian-script signage.

## Victorian

**Feels:** ornate, literary, gothic. **Use for:** books, spirits, apothecary and
heritage brands, tattoo and occult themes. **Not for:** tech or minimal brands.

- **Palette:** engraving black on cream; or deep burgundy, bottle green, brass, candle
  gold. Approx., gothic interior: burgundy `#5E1A24` (dominant) · bottle green
  `#1F3B2D` (secondary) · brass `#B08D3C` (accent) · candle gold `#E8B85A` (accent) ·
  soot black `#141110` (shadow).
- **Light:** candle, gas lamp, window; low key.
- **Medium & texture:** steel engraving lines, aged paper, foxing, embossed ornament.
- **Shots:** flat frontal (label, plate), environmental portrait, profile.
- **Composition:** symmetrical frames and cartouches, ornamental borders, centered
  subject.
- **Staging depth:** flat for engraving and ornamental work; full for a gothic photo
  (candle or lamp as the source, low key).
- **Type:** ornate display serifs, small caps, decorative initials.
- **Variants:**
  - *Engraving* — fine line engraving, crosshatch shading, scientific-plate feel.
  - *Gothic* — dark, candlelit, cathedral arches, ravens, mystery.
  - *Ornamental* — flourishes, labels, borders, apothecary bottles.
- **Engines:** Midjourney for engraving textures; GPT-Image for labels with real text.
- **Slop risks:** steampunk gears added by default, sepia filter on a modern image.

## Bohemian

**Feels:** earthy, free, layered. **Use for:** lifestyle, festival, craft, wellness,
home textiles. **Not for:** corporate, tech, minimal.

- **Palette:** terracotta, rust, ochre, sage, cream, indigo. Approx.: terracotta
  `#C46A47` (dominant) · cream `#F1E6D2` (secondary) · ochre `#CC9A3B` (secondary) ·
  sage `#9CAF88` (accent) · rust `#A0472A` (accent) · indigo `#2E3A6B` (shadow).
- **Light:** warm golden hour, dappled sun through leaves or macramé.
- **Medium & texture:** woven textiles, rattan, linen, dried flowers, hand-drawn
  pattern.
- **Shots:** candid mid-action, overhead flat-lay, environmental portrait.
- **Composition:** layered and relaxed, off-center, plants framing the edges.
- **Staging depth:** full for the *boho lifestyle photo* (dappled sun, textiles at real
  sizes, people at ease); flat for textile and folk pattern.
- **Type:** handwritten or a soft serif.
- **Variants:**
  - *Textile illustration* — flat earthy pattern illustrations, block-print motifs.
  - *Boho lifestyle photo* — sunlit interiors, rugs, plants, people at ease.
  - *Folk pattern* — repeating folk motifs in limited inks.
- **Engines:** all families; Nano Banana and Flux for photo, Midjourney for pattern.
- **Slop risks:** pampas grass plus macramé plus arch in every frame; cultural motifs
  used as decoration without meaning — be specific and respectful.

## Art Deco

**Feels:** glamorous, geometric, golden. **Use for:** hospitality, events, spirits,
premium invitations. **Not for:** casual or eco-earthy brands.

- **Palette:** black, cream and gold; or emerald, navy and gold. Approx.: black
  `#0E0E0E` (dominant, shadow) · cream `#F1E8D0` (secondary) · gold `#C9A24A` (accent)
  · or emerald `#0E5C46` and navy `#14213D` (dominant, secondary).
- **Light:** flat for poster; for photo, theatrical spot and gleam on metal.
- **Medium & texture:** metallic lines, sunburst rays, stepped forms, lacquer.
- **Shots:** symmetric center-punch, low-angle hero (buildings), flat frontal.
- **Composition:** strict symmetry, vertical emphasis, frames of repeated lines.
- **Staging depth:** flat for the poster; full for a photograph (a theatrical spot,
  gleam on metal, the building's verticals kept vertical).
- **Type:** geometric display with high waists and thin-thick contrast.
- **Variants:**
  - *Gold and black geometric* — sunbursts, fans, stepped frames.
  - *Travel poster* — flat illustrated landscapes and liners with streamlined forms.
- **Engines:** Midjourney excels; GPT-Image or Ideogram for poster text.
- **Slop risks:** gold everything, Gatsby party cliché, fake decorative text.
