# Directions — Mixed and texture

Three directions built from layering, surfaces and excess. Shot recipe names refer to
[shots.md](../shots.md); grid terms to [composition.md](../composition.md).

**For all three:** the risk is noise, not blandness. State a hierarchy — one element
that reads first — and how much of the frame stays quieter.

## Contents

- Collage
- Graffiti
- Maximalism

---

## Collage

**Feels:** layered, editorial, rebellious. **Use for:** covers, campaigns, music, zines,
editorial features. **Not for:** clean product shots or calm UI backgrounds.

- **Palette:** black-and-white photo cutouts plus two or three flat color shapes; or
  faded vintage print color.
- **Light:** each cutout keeps its own light; flat paper shadows between layers.
- **Medium & texture:** torn and scissor-cut edges, tape, paper grain, halftone in
  photos, visible layering.
- **Shots:** mixed — a cutout face from a close-up beside a tiny wide-shot figure.
  Scale contrast is the point.
- **Composition:** a clear focal cutout; diagonal arrangement; elements breaking the
  frame edge.
- **Type:** cut-out letters, ransom-note mix, or one bold sans.
- **Variants:**
  - *Cut paper* — scissor-cut colored paper and photo pieces, hand-arranged.
  - *Photo zine* — photocopy black and white, high contrast, tape and staples.
  - *Digital mixed media* — clean cutouts, flat shapes and grain, a polished editorial
    collage.
- **Engines:** every family can assemble it; GPT-Image follows exact arrangements best.
  Never collage real people's photos or copyrighted images the user doesn't own.
- **Slop risks:** random vintage ephemera, butterflies and flowers sprinkled in,
  stamps and postmarks everywhere.

## Graffiti

**Feels:** street, loud, urban. **Use for:** youth, music, sport, events, streetwear.
**Not for:** corporate trust, healthcare, luxury (unless subverting it).

- **Palette:** spray-paint brights with black outlines and white highlights; or a
  stencil's one or two colors on concrete.
- **Light:** daylight or a streetlight on a real wall.
- **Medium & texture:** overspray, drips, brick and concrete texture, layers of older
  paint and posters underneath.
- **Shots:** flat frontal wall, three-quarter along the wall, wide environmental with
  a person for scale.
- **Composition:** the piece fills the wall; letters overlapping; a character beside.
- **Type:** wildstyle or bubble letters — legible if the word matters.
- **Variants:**
  - *Mural piece* — a large painted wall with lettering and characters.
  - *Sticker and tag* — layered stickers, marker tags, poster scraps.
  - *Stencil* — sharp stencilled images in one or two colors.
- **Engines:** GPT-Image or Ideogram if the word must read. Midjourney for the wall
  texture.
- **Slop risks:** unreadable letters, a real artist's tag, generic "street" brick
  wall with a hoodie.

## Maximalism

**Feels:** joyful, abundant, bold. **Use for:** fashion, beauty, events, social,
lifestyle brands with confidence. **Not for:** minimal tech, finance, healthcare.

- **Palette:** many saturated colors with a governing scheme (jewel tones, candy
  brights); patterns that share a color.
- **Light:** bright and even, or a hard flash, so every pattern reads.
- **Medium & texture:** pattern on pattern, rich materials (velvet, lacquer, tile),
  ornaments.
- **Shots:** flat frontal, symmetric center-punch, overhead flat-lay.
- **Composition:** the frame filled edge to edge; a central subject that still reads
  first by scale or contrast.
- **Type:** high-contrast display serif or a chunky decorative face.
- **Variants:**
  - *Pattern clash* — florals, stripes and checks combined on purpose.
  - *Dopamine color* — big blocks of saturated color, playful shapes.
  - *Eclectic still life* — an abundant set: objects, plants, fabrics, ornaments.
- **Engines:** Midjourney strongest; `--s` higher is fine. For seamless patterns,
  Midjourney V7 with `--tile`. GPT-Image for exact object lists.
- **Slop risks:** clutter with no focus, everything the same saturation, pink and gold
  by default.
